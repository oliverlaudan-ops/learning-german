5. REVIEW SCOPE

The currently committed inventory is in audio-inventory.json. It identifies
45 MP3 files by Git blob SHA and size; it is not a historic synthesis receipt.
The current generator documents voices for Sets 2-4; the legacy Set 1
recordings have no exact per-turn synthesis receipt. No waveforms or learner
data were altered by this notice update.

Unresolved: unrestricted publication/reuse clearance for the Thorsten/Lessac
and Kerstin/Ryan model chains. A notice cannot substitute for permission.
To close this point, obtain a suitable written grant/legal assessment or
regenerate affected recordings with models whose full training provenance
and intended-use permissions are documented. New models/versions need
a new review. This is a source review, not a legal certification.
